Everything a good receptionist does

On the cell phone you already carry. Set it up once, and change your greeting and choices whenever you like.

Answers every call

Your receptionist greets callers in your own voice, day or night.

Sends each call to the right place

The right person, your booking page, your price list or your opening hours.

Rings your cell

Only when the call is really for you, on the phone you already have.

Takes messages

Callers leave a voicemail and you get an alert on your phone to play it.

Knows your hours

After closing time, callers hear your after-hours message and leave a message.

Rings your whole team

Add a partner’s phone or your laptop. They all ring; the first person free answers.

Works from your website

One Call button on your site, or a link and QR code for Google Maps, Facebook or your door.

Keeps your number private

Callers never see your cell number, and calls are never recorded.

No phone line to pay

$30 a year per line after the free beta. No per-minute charges.

AI receptionist Coming soon

Answers common questions for you, like your hours, prices and directions, and sums up your messages.

Set up in three steps

You set it all up yourself, from your browser.

  1. 1
    Record your greeting

    Say hello and set the choices: press 1 for bookings, 2 to talk to you. Record it from your browser.

  2. 2
    Install the app on your phone

    Open a link on your phone and add it to your home screen. Your phone number stays the same.

  3. 3
    Add the button to your site

    Paste one line into WordPress, Wix, Squarespace or Shopify. Can’t edit your site? Share your call link or a QR code.
