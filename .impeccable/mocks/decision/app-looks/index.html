Three looks for the minivoip app

One app for the phone and the computer. Each look shows the phone home screen, the phone ringing, and the computer window. Pick one, or mix parts.

A · One list, light B · Status first, dark C · Tabs, warm light
Look A

One list, light

Everything in one clean list, with an “Available” switch on top. On the computer it’s a small window you park in a corner; a call shows up as a dark card inside it.

Look B

Status first, dark

A big “You’re available” card with On, Pause 1 h and Off, then only what needs you. On the computer it’s a wide window like Google Chat: menu, calls, and the ringing call big on the right.

Look C

Tabs like a phone, warm light

Tabs at the bottom like a real phone app. The ringing screen says it in plain words. On the computer: a wide window with a voicemail open and the call popping up in the corner.

About these pictures. Joe’s B&B and its calls are a demo. Only the Android phone gets a full ringing screen; an iPhone shows a short alert instead (Apple’s rule for web apps). Nothing here is wired up yet.
